I am a computer vision researcher. Most of my explorations draws inspiration from the ingenuity of human brain. Its ability to improve itself with experiences, and its natural instinct to be creative guides my research in multi-modal learning and generative modeling with neural networks. I obtained my PhD and Master’s degree from IIT Hyderabad under the guidance of Prof. Vineeth N Balasubramanian.
